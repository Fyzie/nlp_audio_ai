Pump 204 requires bearing lubrication every 400 operating hours using synthetic lithium grease (grade NLGI 2).
Inspect the primary intake valve on Pump 204 weekly for cavitation damage and seal integrity.
Conveyor Belt B tension must be maintained at exactly 50 lbs to prevent slippage. Tension adjustments should only be performed while the main motor is locked out and tagged out.
Conveyor Belt A handles heavy raw materials; check the vulcanized splices every 30 days for fraying or structural weakness.
If the hydraulic press temperature exceeds 90C, immediately engage the emergency cooling system and halt the feed mechanism.
The hydraulic press requires hydraulic fluid replacement every 12 months or when viscosity drops below 40 cSt.
Air compressor units C-1 and C-2 must be drained of condensation daily to prevent internal rusting and pressure fluctuations.
For all electrical control panels, perform thermal imaging inspections quarterly to identify overheating contactors or loose terminal blocks.
In the event of a total power failure, the backup diesel generator will automatically engage within 10 seconds. Do not manually restart high-load machinery until stable grid power is confirmed by the control room.
All operators must wear Class 2 arc flash suits when servicing the main switchgear in Sector 4.
Robotic Welding Arm R-7 requires daily calibration of its optical sensors using the standard calibration grid.
